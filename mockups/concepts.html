CareLoop: three directions

The same content three ways: the dashboard a patient lands on, and the check-in screen where the phone call happens. Each direction is shown at 1280 wide and at 400 wide. Scroll the wide boards sideways on a narrow screen.

A. The Clinical Record B. The Day C. The Consumer Health App

Concept A

The Clinical Record

Optimises for
Trust and completeness. Everything about today is on one screen, in a table, in one glance, and a daughter or a pharmacist can read it over the patient's shoulder without scrolling.
Gives up
Warmth, and a little of the calm that space buys. Density means the primary action has to fight for its place rather than owning the screen.
Disclosures live
In the record itself as data (source, time read, what could not be read), and in a three column small print foot. The interaction is a record entry with a heavy rule, not a banner.

Concept B

The Day

Optimises for
Making the grouping obvious and the phone call the spine of the product. The day runs down the page, each call is an event on it, and the doses hang off the call that covers them, so three calls for five doses is the shape of the screen rather than a sentence about it.
Gives up
The at a glance medicine list. If you want to answer "what am I on", you read the day rather than a list, and a medicines page has to carry that instead.
Disclosures live
Attached to the thing they qualify. The interaction is pinned to the 6:00 PM call and marked on the morning Aspirin dose, because that is where it is true. The general disclosures sit past the end of the day, after the last event, where the spine runs out.

Concept C

The Consumer Health App

Optimises for
One clear next action for someone holding a phone without reading glasses. Large type, generous space, a single decision per screen, and the day reduced to three plain lines.
Gives up
Density and simultaneity. A carer wanting the whole picture scrolls, and the record detail moves to pages behind this one.
Disclosures live
One line under the primary action for the thing that is true right now, one full width band for the interaction placed after the day and before the appointment, and a plain worded "What CareLoop does not do" section at the foot with headings you can read without opening anything.
